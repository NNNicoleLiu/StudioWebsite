Privacy Policy for Mia’s Jianbing Stand

Mahua Game Studio (“we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and handled when you play Mia’s Jianbing Stand (“the Game”).

Last updated · October 7, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the Game and the services available on your device, the Game and its service providers may collect the following types of information.

Account and Contact Information

The Game uses Unity Authentication and may use Apple Game Center for player authentication.

Information associated with authentication services may include:

  • User ID or other account identifiers
  • Email address
  • Other user contact information
  • Game Center account information made available through Apple’s services

The Game supports anonymous authentication where applicable. If you connect your account to Apple Game Center, authentication information may be associated with your Game Center account.

Gameplay and User Content

We may collect information necessary to provide game features, including:

  • Game progress and saved game data
  • Gameplay-related information
  • Leaderboard scores and statistics
  • Player display names
  • Player-name reports and related moderation information
  • Other information you provide through game features

Player display names and leaderboard information may be visible to other players.

Location Information

Our services or third-party SDKs may collect coarse location information, such as an approximate location derived from network or device information.

The Game does not request your precise GPS location for gameplay.

Usage and Diagnostic Information

The Game and its service providers may collect technical information necessary to operate, maintain, analyse, and improve the Game, including:

  • Other usage data
  • Crash data
  • Performance data
  • Other diagnostic data
  • Device identifiers

This information may be used for app functionality, analytics, troubleshooting, performance monitoring, fraud prevention, and maintaining the reliability of the Game.

2. How We Use Information

We may use collected information to:

  • Authenticate players and maintain player accounts
  • Save and restore game progress
  • Provide leaderboards and multiplayer features
  • Operate gameplay features
  • Process and moderate player display-name reports
  • Maintain the security and integrity of the Game
  • Diagnose crashes and technical problems
  • Monitor and improve performance
  • Analyse how the Game and its services operate
  • Provide customer support
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not use the data described in this policy to track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.

3. Apple Game Center

The Game may integrate with Apple Game Center.

When you use Game Center, Apple may process information according to Apple's own privacy practices. Information provided through Game Center may be used by the Game to authenticate you and provide Game Center-related functionality.

Your use of Game Center is also subject to Apple's applicable terms and privacy policies.

4. Unity Services

The Game uses Unity services to provide backend functionality. These services may include:

  • Unity Authentication
  • Unity Cloud Save
  • Unity Leaderboards
  • Unity Cloud Code
  • Unity Multiplayer-related services

These services may process identifiers, gameplay information, saved game data, usage information, and other information necessary to provide their functionality.

Unity processes information according to its own applicable privacy policies and service terms.

5. Advertising

The Game may display advertisements using Unity LevelPlay and integrated advertising services, including Unity Ads.

Advertising and advertising-quality services may process device and technical information to deliver, operate, measure, secure, and monitor advertisements.

Based on the configuration of the Game, we do not use collected data to track you across apps and websites owned by other companies.

The availability and type of advertising may vary depending on your location, device settings, age-related requirements, consent choices, and applicable law.

6. In-App Purchases

The Game offers in-app purchases.

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. Mahua Game Studio does not directly receive or store your full payment card or banking information.

Apple handles payment information according to its own terms and privacy practices.

The Game may receive information necessary to confirm purchases, restore eligible purchases, and provide purchased content.

7. Data Sharing and Service Providers

We use third-party service providers to operate features of the Game, including authentication, cloud storage, multiplayer functionality, leaderboards, advertising, analytics, diagnostics, and purchases.

These providers may process information on our behalf or under their own applicable privacy terms.

We do not sell your personal information.

8. Account and Data Deletion

Where account deletion is available in the Game, you may request deletion using the account deletion option provided in the Game.

Deleting an account may permanently remove game data associated with that account, such as saved progress and other account-related information, subject to technical, legal, security, fraud-prevention, or regulatory retention requirements.

Some information may also be processed independently by third-party services such as Apple. Data controlled independently by those services may need to be managed through the relevant provider.

Account deletion may be permanent and may not be reversible.

9. Children’s Privacy

Mia’s Jianbing Stand is a general-audience game and is not specifically directed to children.

We do not knowingly design the Game to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable privacy laws.

Where age-related privacy requirements apply, we and our service providers may use age-related information, device settings, regional information, or other mechanisms to apply appropriate privacy protections.

10. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures, together with the security features provided by our service providers, to protect information processed through the Game.

However, no electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. International Data Processing

Our service providers may process information in countries or regions other than the country where you live.

Where required, information is handled in accordance with applicable privacy and data-protection requirements.

12. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights regarding your personal information, such as the right to request access, correction, or deletion of certain information.

You may also be able to control certain privacy permissions through your device, Apple account, Game Center settings, or other platform settings.

For privacy-related requests concerning information controlled by Mahua Game Studio, contact us using the details below.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Game, our services, or applicable legal requirements change.

When we make changes, we will update the “Last Updated” date at the top of this policy.
